The Golden Era

In the 1970s, Venice Beach became the epicenter of the bodybuilding universe. Arnold Schwarzenegger, Lou Ferrigno, Franco Columbu, and a generation of icons turned Gold's Gym into a global phenomenon.

Pumping Iron

When the cameras rolled for Pumping Iron in 1977, the world saw what Venice already knew: this was where champions were made. The documentary forever cemented Gold's Gym as the most famous gym on Earth.

A Living Legacy

Today, the walls of Gold's Gym Venice carry the spirit of every athlete who trained here. From golden era bodybuilders to modern-day competitors, the Mecca remains hallowed ground.
